Genetics clinic, first visit. A 3-year-old girl referred for developmental delay.

In the newborn period she had prolonged jaundice, with vomiting and feeding difficulties. She was floppy as an infant. At 14 months she had a generalized tonic-clonic seizure. Her speech is delayed: she says a few single words. Hearing screening was normal, and there are no cataracts.

Laboratory studies showed a raised blood galactose, and her erythrocyte galactose-1-phosphate uridylyltransferase activity was markedly reduced.

Her parents are healthy. A maternal cousin has epilepsy.
